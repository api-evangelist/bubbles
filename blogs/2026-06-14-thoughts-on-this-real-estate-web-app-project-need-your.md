I’ve actually built something in this space recently, so I’ll share my honest take from going through it. A real estate web app like Zillow (listings, search, filters, messaging, dashboards, etc.) is absolutely possible in Bubble at MVP or even early production stage. The real limitation isn’t “can you build it?” — it’s how you structure it for scale from day one .
