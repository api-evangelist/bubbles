That changes how fixes should be prioritised: not by how hard they are, but by how many users hit them and where in the journey. Most of what I do is exactly this kind of work: live Bubble apps with real users, where the founder is not technical and needs someone who explains what is broken in plain language, says what it costs to fix, and does not turn a small issue into a big project.
