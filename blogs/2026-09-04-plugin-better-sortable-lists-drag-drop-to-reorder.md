Hi everyone I’m excited to launch Better Sortable Lists , a plugin for adding fast and reliable drag-and-drop sorting to Repeating Groups. It supports large lists with lazy loading, nested Repeating Groups, transfers between lists, drag handles and offers two saving strategies. I’ve tested it with more than 1,000 items without encountering performance issues.
