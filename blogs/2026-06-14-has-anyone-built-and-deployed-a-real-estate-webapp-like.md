A Zillow style MVP for a city or local market is definitely possible in Bubble. Most of the core features (maps, filters, saved listings, dashboards, messaging) can be built without major issues. From what I’ve seen, the bigger challenge is usually database structure and handling large amounts of listing data efficiently, rather than Bubble itself.
