A Bubble forum thread on best practices for building multi-language apps. Replies recommend the App Text feature (which has no workload unit cost and only needs a refresh when switching languages), using langString variables for option sets and Google Sheets for translation, and clarify how App Text language selection works for native mobile apps based on device settings.
